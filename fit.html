02 / MEASUREMENT ESSENTIALS

Get it
right.

Use a flexible tape, stand naturally and keep it level. For best results, have someone assist you.

ASK ABOUT YOUR FIT ↗
  1. CHEST

    Measure around the fullest part of the chest without pulling the tape tightly.

  2. SHOULDERS

    Measure between the outer shoulder points across the back.

  3. WAIST

    Measure around the natural waist while breathing normally.

  4. INSEAM

    Measure from the inner upper leg to the preferred trouser hem.

DISCUSS YOUR FIT ↗
FITTING QUESTIONS

Good fit
starts here.

Should I measure myself?

Measurements are more consistent when a second person helps. Keep the measuring tape flat and comfortably snug.

How do I choose between the three fits?

Choose Slim for a close silhouette, Tailored for balanced shaping or Relaxed for more room. Your preferred styling and comfort matter just as much as the measurement.

Do you offer standard or bespoke sizing?

This concept presents both fit guidance and a bespoke enquiry journey. Exact product sizing and alteration services should be confirmed before a real order is placed.

Can I request help selecting a size?

Yes. Use the enquiry page to describe your preferred fit and occasion. This website is currently a demonstration, so its forms are not yet linked to a live booking provider.
