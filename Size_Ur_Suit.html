01 / MEASURE WITH CONFIDENCE

Bespoke Measurements

Use a soft measuring tape and keep it comfortably snug, not tight. For best results, ask someone to help you measure.

01

Shoulders

Measure across the back from one shoulder point to the other.

02

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level.

03

Waist

Measure around your natural waist without pulling the tape tight.

04

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips.

05

Back Length

Measure from the base of your neck to your desired jacket hem.

06

Inseam

Measure along the inside leg to your preferred trouser hem.

07

Sleeves

Measure from the shoulder point to the wrist with your arm slightly bent.

08

Trouser Length

Measure from the waistband to your preferred trouser hem.
